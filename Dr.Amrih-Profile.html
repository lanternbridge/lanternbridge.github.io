About

Guiding students from first question
to first day on campus.

I am an education consultant and university lecturer who helps Indonesian and international students secure admission and scholarships at leading universities in China.

My own journey began in Boyolali, Central Java, and led to a Ph.D. in Chinese Philology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. Along the way I learned first-hand how opaque the application process can feel from the outside — which universities genuinely fit, how to write a personal statement that stands out, which scholarships are realistic, and how to prepare documents that survive scrutiny.

Since then I have spent more than two decades teaching Mandarin, English and Indonesian across universities in Indonesia and China — and working one-to-one with students and families making that same journey. I prepare candidates for HSK 1–6, guide them through CSC and university scholarship applications, and stay with them until they are settled on campus.

What I do

End-to-end support for studying in China.

University & Programme Matching

An honest shortlist based on your grades, budget, language level and career goal — not on commission.

Admission Applications

Personal statements, recommendation letters, transcripts, notarisation and portal submission — prepared properly, on time.

Scholarship Strategy

CSC, provincial, Confucius Institute and university-level scholarships — matched to your profile and documented correctly.

HSK 1–6 Preparation

Structured coaching in listening, reading, writing and speaking — built on years of teaching Chinese as a foreign language.

Visa & Pre-Departure

JW202, X1/X2 visa paperwork, medical checks, accommodation and a realistic briefing on daily life in China.

Student Mentoring & Aftercare

Support after arrival — registration, course selection, campus adjustment and staying in touch with families.
