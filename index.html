Case studyStudi kasus

From four failed suppliers to a factory partnership. Dari empat pemasok gagal menjadi kemitraan pabrik.

A furniture importer in Surabaya needed more than another sourcing agent — they needed a team on the ground. Here is how we crossed. Seorang importir furnitur di Surabaya membutuhkan lebih dari sekadar agen sourcing — mereka butuh tim di lapangan. Inilah cara kami menyeberang.

The clientKlien

An Indonesian furniture importer based in Surabaya, expanding from residential pieces into modular office furniture for the local B2B market. Importir furnitur Indonesia yang berbasis di Surabaya, mengembangkan lini dari furnitur residensial menjadi furnitur kantor modular untuk pasar B2B lokal.

The challengeTantangan

After three failed attempts through online aggregators, the client had paid two deposits to factories that could not deliver the agreed specification — and lost a container to customs delays. They did not need another email broker. They needed a partner on the ground. Setelah tiga kali gagal melalui agregator daring, klien telah membayar dua deposit ke pabrik yang tidak dapat memenuhi spesifikasi yang disepakati — dan kehilangan satu kontainer karena penundaan bea cukai. Mereka tidak membutuhkan perantara email lagi. Mereka membutuhkan mitra di lapangan.

Our approachPendekatan kami
  1. Week 1–2 · Requirement mapping.Minggu 1–2 · Pemetaan kebutuhan. We translated the client's specification sheets into Mandarin manufacturing standards and shortlisted seven candidate factories in Guangzhou and Foshan. Kami menerjemahkan lembar spesifikasi klien ke standar manufaktur Mandarin dan menyaring tujuh kandidat pabrik di Guangzhou dan Foshan.
  2. Week 3 · On-site visits.Minggu 3 · Kunjungan langsung. Our team walked the floors of all seven and filtered them to three finalists based on capacity, certifications and MOQ flexibility. Tim kami mengunjungi ketujuh pabrik dan menyaringnya menjadi tiga finalis berdasarkan kapasitas, sertifikasi, dan fleksibilitas MOQ.
  3. Week 4 · Negotiation.Minggu 4 · Negosiasi. MOQ reduced from 1,200 to 780 units; unit price down 22%. Payment terms restructured to 30/40/30 with milestone inspections. MOQ turun dari 1.200 menjadi 780 unit; harga satuan turun 22%. Skema pembayaran direstrukturisasi menjadi 30/40/30 dengan inspeksi bertahap.
  4. Week 5–6 · Pre-shipment inspection.Minggu 5–6 · Inspeksi pra-pengiriman. Two units failed quality control and were replaced before the container was sealed. Shipped on schedule. Dua unit gagal kontrol kualitas dan diganti sebelum kontainer disegel. Dikirim sesuai jadwal.
